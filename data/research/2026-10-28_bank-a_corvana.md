Corvana 2035s have widened by around 75bp this month to about 370bp over Treasuries, as the market prices a downgrade
following the negative outlook. We still see downside: the fiscal slippage is not yet in the numbers. We recommend
buying 5y CDS protection and moving underweight hard-currency debt versus Valdoria.

Politics: the coalition holds a two-seat majority, and the budget vote on 12 November is the next flashpoint.
