source: Bank B

We expect the MPC to hold Bank Rate on 5 November with a 6-3 vote, but with dovish guidance. With 10y gilts around
4.5%, we prefer receiving 10y SONIA versus paying 2y, and we see 10y SONIA swap spreads (now around -57bp) as too
negative given the reduction in long-end gilt supply in the remit revision.

Risks: a hawkish surprise on services inflation would hit the front end hardest.
